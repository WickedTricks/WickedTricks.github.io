Add it to Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on a computer and switch to wickedtrickspress@gmail.com.
  2. Click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Wicked Tricks".
  4. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes. Send yourself a test email.
